# Milestone 2 — live check guide

One full walk of Milestone 2 on **https://thirtydayhomes.com**. Do the parts in
order. Each step says where to go, who to be, what you should see, and one
thing to try that should *not* work. Take a screenshot wherever it says 📸
and send them all in one message at the end.

**Before you start**

- Press **Ctrl + Shift + R** on each page the first time, so you see the newest version.
- "Viewing as" is the dark bar at the top of every page (sign in as the admin first).
  Pick a person there to see the site as them.
- **Do not** approve or reject a real landlord's home, and **do not** send an enquiry
  to a real landlord. Use the TEST homes and the "Active landlord" persona's own home.
- Phone check: in Chrome press **F12**, then **Ctrl + Shift + M**, pick an iPhone.

---

## 1. Finding a home (renter)

Viewing as: **Renter**

1. Open https://thirtydayhomes.com/homes/
   - You see homes, the filter bar, and "N homes" above them.
2. Put **1000** in Min price and **2500** in Max price, press **Show homes**.
   - Only homes in that price range; two labels appear above them. 📸
3. Press the **×** on one label. That filter goes; the other stays.
4. Set **Sort by → Price: low to high**. Cheapest first.
5. Type **Oakland** in the search box, press **Search**.
   - Pittsburgh homes in Oakland — **never** "Oakland, CA". 📸
6. Type **15226**, press **Search**.
   - "N homes within 15 miles of 15226", nearest first, each saying how far. 📸
7. Choose a hospital in **Near a hospital**, press **Show homes**.
   - Nearest to that hospital first; each card shows the distance to it.
8. Press **Map** (right of the count). The homes appear as price pills on a map. 📸
- **Try to break it:** put **90000** in Min price. The page says no homes cost that
  much and offers to clear it — it never shows every home again. 📸
- **Dates:** on the home page https://thirtydayhomes.com/ pick a move-in and a
  move-out 9 days later. It says stays are 30 days or longer.
- **Phone:** repeat step 1 on iPhone. The filters sit behind a **Filters** button;
  the home page date search stacks neatly. 📸

## 2. A home's page (renter)

Viewing as: **Renter**

1. Open any home from the list.
   - Photos with **Show all photos**, the price box with fees, **Everything you need**,
     the availability calendar, **Close to care** with three hospitals and a
     neighbourhood **circle** (never a pin), and **Enquire about this home**. 📸
- **Try to break it:** look for the street address anywhere on the page. It must not be there.
- **Phone:** the page reads top to bottom, nothing runs off the side. 📸

## 3. Sending an enquiry (renter → landlord)

Viewing as: **Renter** — on the **Active landlord** persona's home only.

1. Fill in the form with **your own email**, tick the box, press **Send enquiry**.
   - The form is replaced by a green **Message sent** naming the home. 📸
2. Switch to **Active landlord**, open https://thirtydayhomes.com/account/?view=inquiries
   - The message is there, marked unread. Open it: the renter's details show. 📸
3. Press **Archive**; the **Archived** tab brings it back.
- **Try to break it:** press Send with the name empty. It asks for the name and keeps
  everything else you typed.
- Texts to landlords are in test mode until Rob's proof arrives, so only Rob's own
  number receives one for now.

## 4. A landlord's listings

Viewing as: **Active landlord**

1. Open https://thirtydayhomes.com/account/?view=listings
   - Each home shows its status in words, one line explaining it, and one main button. 📸
2. On a live home: **Pause** → it disappears for renters, the button becomes **Resume**.
   **Resume** → live again.
3. **Availability** on a home: add a period, save. The message confirms it; the home's
   page calendar crosses those days out.
4. **Edit** a live home: change the rent, **Continue** → "Anything you changed here is live now."
   Change the title → a page explains it goes back to review (use **Go back and discard changes**).
- **Try to break it:** press **Delete** on a home, then **Keep it**. Nothing is deleted.
- **Phone:** the listings page on iPhone, every button easy to tap. 📸

## 5. Adding a home (landlord → staff)

Viewing as: **Active landlord**

1. https://thirtydayhomes.com/add-listing/ — walk the four steps with a test home
   (name it "TEST — delete me"). Step 3: add photos, use the arrows, **Make cover**.
2. Step 4 lists every answer with **Edit** links. Tick the Fair Housing box, **Submit for approval**.
   - "A person reviews it — usually within one business day." 📸
3. Switch to **Administrator**, open https://thirtydayhomes.com/account/?view=listings
   - The TEST home is in **Waiting for approval**. Press **Request changes**, type a note,
     send. (Do not approve a real landlord's home.) 📸
4. Back as **Active landlord**: the note shows on My listings.
- Afterwards delete the TEST home.

## 6. A payment fails (membership)

Viewing as: **Administrator** → https://thirtydayhomes.com/account/?view=members

1. Open **Active landlord**, set status **Past due**, **Save member**.
   Switch to **Active landlord**: a red panel says the homes stay visible until a date,
   with **Update your card**. 📸
2. As Administrator set **Expired**, save. As **Renter**, that landlord's home now says
   it is not available, and it is gone from Find a home. As **Active landlord**: "Your 1
   home is hidden … Nothing is deleted." 📸
3. As Administrator set **Active** with an expiry next year, save. As **Active landlord**:
   "Payment received — your home is back online." As **Renter** the home is back. 📸
- **Important:** end with **Active landlord** set back to **Active**.

## 7. New landlord email confirmation

Signed out (or a private window)

1. https://thirtydayhomes.com/register/ — sign up with an email you can open.
   - The dashboard says one step is left; a yellow **Confirm your email address** panel. 📸
2. Open the email from ThirtyDayHomes, click the link.
   - "Email confirmed", the yellow panel is gone. 📸
- **Try to break it:** click the same link again. "Already confirmed."
- Delete this test account afterwards (Administrator → Members).

## 8. Staff screens

Viewing as: **Administrator**

1. https://thirtydayhomes.com/account/?view=members — expand a member, click the word
   **Email**: the cursor jumps into the box.
2. https://thirtydayhomes.com/account/?view=facilities — the hospitals, each with its location.
3. https://thirtydayhomes.com/account/?view=inquiries — every enquiry with its delivery status in words. 📸

## 9. Elementor blocks

1. Edit any page with Elementor, search the left panel for **Search results** and
   **Nearby hospitals**. Drag one in: it shows real homes / hospitals in the editor. 📸
   Close without saving.
